About this prototype

This is a minimally functional prototype built for academic demonstration (Assignment 2, AI Use-Case Design and Feasibility). It uses Tesseract.js (client-side OCR) to read text from an uploaded photo and compares it against a manually entered expected identifier — simulating the AI Verification Engine described in the AudiTag invention disclosure. It runs entirely in your browser; no data is uploaded to any server.
